How to play

  • Tap two numbers that are equal or add up to 10.
  • They must touch in a row, column or diagonal. Removed cells don't block the way.
  • Stuck? Tap Add row to add 9 more numbers. You get 5 per game.
  • Clear every number to win. If no pairs are left and no rows remain, the game is over.
